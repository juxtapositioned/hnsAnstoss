## Spielidee

### Mittelfeldsituation

Offensiv taktische Optionen:
- Links; Mitte; Rechts + Angriffskarte

Defensive taktische Optionen:
- 1 von 3 Optionen setzen auf Links, Mitte; Rechts um den Ball zu klauen und zu kontern
- 2 von 3 Optionen: fallen lassen
- \+ Verteidigungskarte

Wird der Ball geklaut, ist die verteidigende Mannschaft vor dem Tor.
xA entscheidet, ob es zur Torchance kommt.

### Sturmsituation

Offensive taktische Optionen:
- Angriffskarte

Defensive taktische Optionen:
- Verteidigungskarte

xG entscheidet, ob ein Tor fällt.

## Prototype: Die Heiligen (2026-10-02)

Playable at `/heilige.html` (link on the draft screen of the main prototype), next to v0.27 and sharing
none of its sim. Engine `src/engine/heilige.ts`, cards `src/engine/data/saints.ts`, screen `src/ui/heilige.ts`,
headless `npm run heilige` (one match as text) / `npm run heilige -- --stats 1000`.

How the idea was read — each point is open:
- **Lanes.** The attackers pick links / Mitte / rechts, the defenders press one lane and drop back on the
  other two. Same lane: the ball is stolen and the defenders go straight to the Sturm (a counter, xG 0.40
  instead of 0.35). Otherwise xA decides whether there is a chance (base 0.5). The lanes have a character:
  through the middle is the short way (+0.1 xA), over a wing while the press is on the other wing there is
  all the room in the world (+0.15 xA). Reading the press right is worth something beyond not losing the ball.
- **Who plays which cards.** Each coach holds three Angriff and three Verteidigung saints and may invoke one
  per situation: Angriff when his side has the ball, Verteidigung when it has not. A saint only counts where
  his number exists: xA in the midfield, xG in the Sturm (Poldi, Jens and Bodo wait for the shot; Matthias
  is midfield only). A played saint goes under the pile and the next one takes his slot in the hand. The two
  benches never share a saint: each gets half of the Angriff and half of the Verteidigung cards.
- **Saints are a choice, not a reflex** (added 2026-10-02 after a playtest: both benches always played their
  biggest saint and 54 % of all duels cancelled to zero). Each bench may call **4 saints a half**. The rival
  decides first and shows his saint with its line; you answer, match, overplay or save yours. He calls his
  biggest in front of goal while he has calls, in the midfield only a 0.5 and only while he has more than 2
  left. On a steal nobody got to invoke anything: the saints go back to the hand and the calls are not used.
- **Randomness and Fußballgott cards are the world's pile.** Nobody plays them; in ~30 % of situations one
  turns over by itself, and its line is said first on the reveal. "+x & −x" is read as a coin: it helps the
  attackers or the defenders. Diego, Gerd and Andreas vom Elfmeterpunkt (+1 xG) always help the attackers,
  Sepp (−1 xG) always the defenders. The Fußballgott cards only have xG, so they only turn up in the Sturm.
- **The numbers add straight onto xA / xG**, kept between 0.03 and 0.95. Cards show their numbers and the
  reveal shows the sum and where the roll landed; the main prototype never shows numbers, this one does
  because the idea is built on them.
- **The rivals have lane tells**, so the guessing can be read: der Schleifer goes and presses through the
  middle (60 %) — until it gets too much for him: stolen twice through the middle, he shouts "Über außen!"
  and attacks over the wings three times; run round twice while pressing the middle, he shouts "Raus auf
  die Flügel!" and presses the wing you last got through, three times. His shout is in the Zeitraffer and
  replaces his tell in the header while it lasts. Der Taktikfuchs never attacks through the same lane twice
  and presses where you last got through (70 %). The pitch shows a dashed trail of exactly that: where they
  last went when they attack, where you last got through when you do.
- **A match** is 7 highlights a half, at least three minutes apart. Whose attack it is: a coin leaned
  against the side that had the last one (35 % repeat), and towards the side that is behind from the 70th.
  Between them the Zeitraffer lines from the main prototype; no line is said twice in a match. No clock:
  the panel waits.

Headless (2000 matches each, `npm run heilige -- --stats 2000 [--rival taktikfuchs] [--reader]`): 3.9 goals a
match, ~4.6 of 14 midfield attacks stolen, ~5 become chances, ~5.7 world cards. A player choosing lanes at
random wins ~38 % against either rival. One who reads the tells (`--reader`) wins 53 % against der Schleifer
when he ignores the shouts (it was 87 % before the Schleifer could change his mind) and 70 % against der
Taktikfuchs. Note: a steal goes straight to a shot, so the side that opens a highlight is more often scored
*against* than for — a third of all attacks become the other side's counter.
